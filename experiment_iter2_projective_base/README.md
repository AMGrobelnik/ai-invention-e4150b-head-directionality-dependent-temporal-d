## Full Summary

This experiment compares real sentence linearizations against 100 random projective baselines per sentence across 86 UD treebanks (41,129 sentences) stratified by WALS word order, language family, and size. IMB profiles are computed and verified via the total_IMB identity (0 violations). Metrics include max_imb, mean_imb, LLF, and parametric convexity costs at alpha in {1.0, 1.2, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0}. Regression-based residualization controls for DD moments and tree properties. The key finding is a monotonically decreasing R-squared of cost from DD features across alpha (0.873 at alpha=1.0 to 0.335 at alpha=3.0, Spearman rho=-1.0), showing temporal overlap contributes beyond DD alone at higher convexity exponents. The negative control passes: R-squared at alpha=1 is high (0.873) with drop of 0.537. Alpha-star is estimated at 2.0 where R-squared from DD first drops below 0.5. Per-treebank results reveal typological variation: head-initial languages like Arabic show higher real LLF than baselines (+0.107), while head-final languages like Japanese show lower real LLF (-0.033). Residualized LLF median Cohen's d is -0.064 with 31% of treebanks exceeding d=0.2. The pipeline uses ProcessPoolExecutor with 4 workers, processing 86 treebanks in 104 seconds. Output includes per-treebank raw and residualized LLF, max_imb, and convexity statistics at each alpha, plus aggregate results with word-order breakdowns and monotonicity verification.
